# Phase 46 — Evidence-First Professional Profile

This phase removes the Conference Gate AI chat surface and replaces the professional profile's unexplained composite index with direct, source-aware professional evidence.

Design principles:
- ConferenceGate-confirmed activity is shown as verified.
- Imported/public evidence remains source-labeled and is not silently promoted to verified activity.
- Self-reported evidence remains separate.
- Opportunity readiness is based on fields organizers actually use for matching: expertise, specialization, research interests, regions and active availability.
- Public evidence research uses the existing Crossref, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, DBLP and official/public-source research pipeline.

User-facing changes:
- AI chat button, modal wiring and retired modal component removed.
- `Conference Gate Index` headline replaced with `Verified Conference Record`.
- Header metrics now show verified conference activity, verified peer reviews, research outputs and verified professional roles.
- `Professional Opportunity Profile` becomes `Opportunity Matching Profile` with actionable readiness gaps.
- Footer now describes source-labeled evidence rather than an unexplained index.
