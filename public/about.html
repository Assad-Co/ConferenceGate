ABOUT CONFERENCEGATE

Connecting the world through conferences.

ConferenceGate brings conference discovery, professional participation, organizer workflows, and trusted records of achievement together in one connected platform.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make meaningful conference participation simpler, more accessible, and more rewarding for professionals, researchers, organizers, and sponsors worldwide. We connect people with relevant events and opportunities, streamline abstract submissions and peer review, and help organizers coordinate the people and processes behind successful conferences.

By bringing these activities into a single platform, we aim to reduce fragmented administration, improve visibility into participation, and help professionals build a credible history of their contributions. We emphasize clear evidence and verification so that achievements such as accepted abstracts, completed reviews, and committee service can be recognized with confidence.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted global gateway for conference engagement and professional recognition: a place where discovery leads naturally to participation, collaboration, and measurable professional growth.

We envision an interconnected conference ecosystem in which professionals can discover opportunities regardless of geography, organizers can run transparent and efficient processes, and sponsors can engage with relevant communities. Over time, we aspire to make verified conference contributions easier to document, share, and value across institutions, disciplines, and borders.

What We Do

  • Conference discovery: Help users explore relevant conferences, calls for papers, and professional opportunities.
  • Abstracts and peer review: Support submissions, review workflows, and status tracking.
  • Organizer collaboration: Provide tools for conference coordination, technical committees, and professional invitations.
  • Professional recognition: Connect eligible activity records to profile achievements, Kudos, and downloadable certificates where the underlying activity is verified.
  • Sponsor connections: Help sponsors and organizers explore relevant partnerships.

Our Commitment to Trust

We believe professional recognition should reflect real activity. ConferenceGate distinguishes submitted information from platform-recorded and organizer-confirmed evidence. Certificates and verified credentials should be based on qualifying records, not simply on registration, invitations, or self-reported claims.

Our goal is to create a more transparent, connected, and valuable conference experience for everyone involved.
