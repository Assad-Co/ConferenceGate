Our Mission

  • Make conference participation simpler, more accessible and more rewarding for professionals, researchers, organizers and sponsors worldwide.
  • Connect people with relevant conferences and professional opportunities.
  • Streamline abstract submissions and peer review while helping organizers manage event operations and workflows.
  • Reduce fragmented administration and help professionals document credible, evidence-based contributions.
✦

Our Vision

  • Become a trusted global gateway for conference engagement and professional recognition.
  • Enable professionals to discover opportunities and collaborate across borders.
  • Empower organizers with transparent workflows and connect sponsors with relevant communities.
  • Make verified professional contributions easier to document, share and recognize across disciplines and institutions.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to get started.

What is ConferenceGate?

ConferenceGate is a platform for discovering conferences and managing professional participation, including abstract submission, peer review and organizer workflows.

Who can use ConferenceGate?

Researchers, industry professionals, reviewers, conference organizers and sponsors can create accounts tailored to their roles.

Can I submit an abstract?

Yes. Browse conferences, review their calls for papers and submit an abstract when submissions are open. Track progress from your account.

Can I organize a conference?

Yes. Organizer workspaces support conference setup, abstract management, reviewer assignments and participant coordination.

How are certificates verified?

Qualifying certificates are linked to recorded activities and can be checked using the platform's certificate verification feature.

Is ConferenceGate free?

Professional accounts are free. Organizer access is currently available during the free-launch period. Sponsor access and additional features may have separate terms.

Our commitment

Recognition built on trust.

Professional achievements should reflect real activity. ConferenceGate distinguishes self-reported information from platform-recorded and organizer-confirmed evidence. Kudos and formal certificates are linked to qualifying activity, not simply to registrations, invitations or unverified claims.
