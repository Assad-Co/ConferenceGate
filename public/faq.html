Frequently Asked Questions

What is ConferenceGate?

ConferenceGate helps professionals discover conferences, manage participation, build verified professional records, and connect with organizers and sponsors.

How do I find a conference?

Open Discover Conferences and search by conference name, acronym, discipline, city, country, organizer, or topic. ConferenceGate also surfaces external worldwide conference results when available.

How do I submit and track an abstract?

Open a conference, choose Submit Abstract, complete the submission form, and track the status from Abstracts. Organizer decisions and review activity appear in your account notifications.

How does the LinkedIn professional-profile import work?

In Edit Profile, add your LinkedIn profile URL and choose Import professional profile. ConferenceGate imports available public professional information such as experience, education, publications, patents, and certifications. Existing profile fields are not silently overwritten.

How do organizers publish a conference?

Organizer accounts can create a conference, add event details, CFP information, deadlines, speakers, committees, and sponsorship information, then publish it so it can appear in ConferenceGate discovery.

How are conference records verified?

ConferenceGate uses source-labeled evidence and official conference sources where available. External results should always be reviewed against the linked official source before making travel, payment, or submission decisions.

How do notifications and messages work?

Notifications open the related ConferenceGate activity when possible. Messaging supports professional, organizer, and sponsor communication within the platform.

Can I correct inaccurate conference information?

Yes. Use Ask Us and include the conference name, official source URL, and the information that needs correction.

Is ConferenceGate free?

Professional access currently includes core discovery and professional-profile features. Organizer and sponsor commercial plans may be introduced or changed separately.

How do I get help with my account?

Open Ask Us from the footer and contact ConferenceGate support. Do not send passwords, recovery codes, or other sensitive credentials.
