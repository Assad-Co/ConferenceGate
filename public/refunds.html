Cancellation & refunds

Clear information about ConferenceGate services, policies and support.

Pre-launch notice: Organizer Pro and Sponsor Pro paid checkout is not yet enabled. The following is the proposed customer policy for review prior to live payments; no payments are currently taken through this page.

Subscription billing

When activated, Organizer Pro and Sponsor Pro are intended to be recurring monthly subscriptions. The final total, any applicable taxes, renewal interval and payment provider will be disclosed at checkout before payment authorization.

Cancellation

Customers will be able to request cancellation through their billing account or by emailing support@conferencegate.com. The effective cancellation date, access through the paid period, and cancellation confirmation will be disclosed during checkout and in billing communications. Do not rely on these proposed terms until checkout activation is announced.

Refunds and billing errors

For duplicate charges, unauthorized transactions, billing errors, or a refund request, contact support@conferencegate.com with your account email and transaction reference. Refund eligibility, renewal rules, and any statutory withdrawal rights will be specified in the final policy and checkout terms before commercial launch. Nothing on this page limits mandatory rights under applicable law.

Payments for sponsorship deals

ConferenceGate subscription fees are distinct from any separate sponsorship transaction negotiated between an organizer and a sponsor. Marketplace settlement and payout services are not offered under these subscription terms.
