Privacy Policy

Data controller. ConferenceGate OÜ, registered address: Sepapaja tn 6, 15551 Tallinn, Harju Maakond, Estonia, operates the ConferenceGate platform. Privacy requests may be submitted using the Ask Us feature. Please do not include passwords or sensitive credentials.

Privacy at ConferenceGate. ConferenceGate uses account and profile information to provide conference discovery, participation, messaging, professional-profile, organizer, and sponsor features.

Information you provide. This may include your name, email, role, organization, professional profile details, conference activity, messages, submissions, reviews, and information you choose to import or publish.

Public professional information. When you request a professional-profile import, ConferenceGate may process publicly available professional information and source-labeled evidence to enrich your profile.

Purposes and lawful bases. Account and platform information is processed to provide requested services, administer accounts, maintain security, and meet applicable legal obligations. Other processing, including optional analytics or marketing, must have an appropriate legal basis and, where required, consent.

Your rights. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You may also lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

Service providers and international transfers. Hosting, authentication, communications, analytics, and payment providers may process information to support the platform. Any international transfer must use a valid legal transfer mechanism where required.

Retention. Information is retained only as long as needed for its stated purposes, contractual commitments, legitimate operational needs, and applicable legal requirements. Specific retention periods should be documented before launch.

Security. ConferenceGate uses authentication and access controls designed to keep private workspaces and account activity separated. Users should protect their own credentials and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

Contact. Privacy questions or requests can be submitted through Ask Us.
